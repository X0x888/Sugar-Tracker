2. Description of Service

Sugar Tracker is a browser extension that displays nutritional information (sugar and salt content) on UK grocery websites. The Extension:

  • Reads nutrition data from supported grocery websites (Waitrose, Ocado, Sainsbury's)
  • Displays colour-coded badges on product images
  • Stores data locally on your device for performance
  • Does not require an account or registration
